Six-domain severity score after Kasai portoenterostomy, scored at one or more follow-up visits, with the course drawn against the derivation cohort.

Research instrument. Not for clinical decision-making.

Fixed at surgery

Two domains are set once and do not change with follow-up: age at Kasai, and the platelet count on the first post-operative day. Together they fix the floor the score cannot fall below.
